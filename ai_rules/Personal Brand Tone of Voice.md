# Personal Brand Tone of Voice

## Core Identity

Ivan is not presented as a designer who creates interfaces.

Ivan is presented as a problem solver who happens to use design as his primary tool.

Curiosity drives the process.
Design refines the outcome.

The brand should communicate intelligence through clarity, never through complexity.

## Positioning

**Not:**
- Creative genius
- Design artist
- Trend follower
- Visual-first designer

**Instead:**
- Strategic thinker
- Systems-minded designer
- Curious problem solver
- Human-centered technologist
- Builder of clear experiences

## Personality

- Curious
- Analytical
- Thoughtful
- Precise
- Calm
- Observant

**Never:**
- Arrogant
- Loud
- Salesy
- Corporate
- Self-congratulatory

## Writing Style

Short sentences.

High signal-to-noise ratio.

Every sentence should either:
- Reveal a belief
- Explain a process
- Demonstrate expertise

Avoid buzzwords whenever possible.

**Prefer:** clarity, systems, questions, patterns, behaviour, understanding, craft

**Over:** innovation, disruption, cutting-edge, revolutionary, passion

## Recurring Themes

- Better questions create better solutions.
- Good design starts with understanding.
- Complexity should feel simple.
- Assumptions deserve scrutiny.
- Technology exists for people.
- Details shape perception.
- Systems influence behaviour.

## Emotional Goal

Visitors should leave with the impression that:

> "This is someone who thinks differently, but methodically."

Not:

> "This is someone trying to convince me that they think differently."

## Tagline

```
Different angles. Better questions.
```
